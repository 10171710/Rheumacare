Our Background

Built to solve the diagnostic delay in chronic joint disease

The average patient suffering from autoimmune symptoms waits between 3 to 5 years and visits multiple physicians before receiving an accurate diagnosis.

In 2008, Dr. Priya Nair founded RheumaCare to break that agonizing cycle. We recognized that patients were being shuffled between distant laboratories, hospital imaging departments, and detached clinics while irreversible joint damage and pain progressed.

By unifying point-of-care ultrasound, specialized serology, in-house infusion suites, and individualized physical rehabilitation under one roof, RheumaCare compressed the diagnostic journey from years into days — giving patients swift relief and lifelong hope.

Guiding Principles

Our Mission, Vision & Values

Our Mission

To transform the standard of rheumatology by delivering rapid, accurate diagnoses, innovative biologic therapies, and holistic lifestyle support — restoring painless mobility, independence, and dignity for every individual we serve.

Our Vision

A healthcare landscape where autoimmune and musculoskeletal conditions are detected in their earliest stages, where patients are never left to navigate chronic pain alone, and where clinical excellence is inseparable from human warmth.

Empathetic Listening

We believe your symptoms even when routine tests are inconclusive. You know your body best, and we listen without rushing.

Scientific Rigor

We deploy proven, evidence-based regimens, international clinical guidelines, and modern targeted biologic protocols, reviewed continuously.

Radical Clarity

No medical jargon. We explain condition mechanisms, treatment trade-offs, laboratory results, and costs with transparent openness.

Continuous Partnership

Chronic conditions evolve over time. We stand beside you through flares, remissions, lifestyle shifts, and milestone victories.
