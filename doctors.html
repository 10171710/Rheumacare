Collaborative Model

Weekly Autoimmune Case Review Rounds

Autoimmune diseases can be elusive and unpredictable. At RheumaCare, our clinicians don't work in clinical silos.

Every Thursday morning, our rheumatologists, clinical immunologists, ultrasound specialists, and infusion nurse leaders convene for our Multidisciplinary Case Conference. We review diagnostic ultrasound imagery, complex antibody profiles, and treatment response trajectories for our most challenging patients.

This rigorous collaborative oversight ensures that every treatment regimen reflects the collective expertise of our entire faculty.
