Clinical Capabilities

Specialized Outpatient Services

Point-of-Care Ultrasound (POCUS)

Real-time high-resolution Power Doppler imaging provides immediate visualization of joint inflammation, synovial thickening, bone erosions, and tendon tears without hospital delays.

  • Immediate bedside results review
  • Zero ionizing radiation exposure
  • Sensitive detection of subclinical synovitis

Outpatient Biologic Infusion Suites

Relaxed, quiet day suites for biologic therapies (Remicade, Actemra, Orencia, Benlysta, Rituxan, Krystexxa) monitored by highly experienced and certified infusion nurses.

  • Heated ergonomic reclining chairs
  • Wi-Fi, refreshments, and calm lighting
  • Anaphylaxis safety protocols

Ultrasound-Guided Joint Injections

Precision needle navigation ensures medications reach the exact anatomical space with minimal discomfort, delivering viscosupplementation (hyaluronic acid) or anti-inflammatory agents.

  • 99% accuracy via ultrasound guidance
  • Synovial fluid aspiration & crystal analysis
  • Knee, hip, shoulder, small joint protocols

Autoimmune Serology & Advanced Lab Testing

Targeted laboratory diagnostics designed specifically for rheumatic diseases, accelerating detection of autoantibodies and verifying organ safety before starting immunotherapy.

  • High-titer ANA, Anti-CCP, RF screening
  • HLA-B27 and complement C3/C4 levels
  • Liver, kidney, hematology monitoring

Rheumatology Physical Therapy

Unlike generic sports physical therapy, our rheumatology rehab focuses on protecting vulnerable articular cartilage, reducing morning stiffness, and building isometric joint stability.

  • Tailored low-impact exercise programs
  • Joint energy-conservation coaching
  • Ergonomic device and splinting guidance

Tele-Rheumatology & Remote Care

Convenient secure video consultations for established patients, allowing medication dosage adjustments, laboratory result reviews, and urgent flare triage without commuting.

  • HIPAA-compliant encrypted video portal
  • Fast remote prescription refills
  • Continuity of care during severe fatigue

Patient FAQ

Questions About Our Services

Clear answers about referrals, procedure preparation, and what to expect during your visit.

Not sure which service you need?

Our clinical navigators can match you to the right specialist and service, Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

If you are unsure, start with a New Patient Comprehensive Consult. A rheumatologist reviews your history, examines your joints, and — where useful — performs same-day point-of-care ultrasound to recommend the most appropriate next step, whether that is laboratory testing, an injection, physical therapy, or infusion therapy.

Self-referrals are welcome for consultations, imaging, and laboratory panels. Advanced procedures — including biologic infusion therapy and ultrasound-guided joint injections — require a rheumatologist prescription or in-office assessment, which we can usually arrange within the same visit.

Most patients describe only mild pressure or a brief sting. We cleanse the skin with a local anesthetic, use real-time ultrasound to guide the needle precisely into the joint, and the appointment typically takes 15 to 20 minutes. You can usually return to light daily activity the same day, with only 24 hours of reduced activity advised for the treated joint.

Infusion sessions generally last from 1.5 to 3 hours depending on the medication. You will be seated in a private day suite with a heated recliner and continuous monitoring by specialty nursing staff. Many patients read, work, or rest comfortably during treatment, and most return to normal routines later the same day.

We work with most major commercial, Medicare, and regional insurance plans. Our benefits verification team confirms your coverage, explains any out-of-pocket costs, and handles prior authorisation for imaging, infusions, and injections before your appointment so there are no billing surprises.
